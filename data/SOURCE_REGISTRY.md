# SIH26083 - Data Source Registry & Provenance

This document establishes the audit trail for all primary datasets, scientific methods, and APIs used in the SIH26083 Human Thermal Stress and Early Warning Prototype.

| ID | Source | URL | Dataset/API | Variables | Resolution | Update | License/Terms | Used For | Limitations |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **SRC-01** | India Meteorological Department (IMD) | [IMD Heatwave Guidance](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/heatwave_guidance.php) | Heat Wave Guidance & Bulletins | Max Temp, Departure from Normal, Heatwave Alerts | District / Synoptic Station | Daily (08:00 & 16:00 IST) | Open Government Data (OGD) India | Climatological thresholds, alert color scheme (Green/Yellow/Orange/Red), heatwave criteria | Station-level data; no programmatic public REST API for raw model grids without formal MoES portal |
| **SRC-02** | NASA Langley Research Center | [NASA POWER API](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/pages/) | Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) API | `T2M` (2m Temp), `RH2M` (Relative Humidity), `WS10M` (10m Wind Speed), `WS2M` (2m Wind Speed), `ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN` (All Sky Solar Radiation) | $0.5^\circ \times 0.625^\circ$ (Met), $1.0^\circ \times 1.0^\circ$ (Solar) | Daily / Hourly near-real-time (NRT) analysis | Open Access / NASA Data Policy | Automated meteorological & solar radiation ingestion pipeline for thermal indices | Reanalysis/assimilation resolution ($\sim 50\text{ km}$); not a substitute for micro-scale urban canopy models |
| **SRC-03** | Census of India (Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner) | [Census Population Finder](https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data/population_finder) | Primary Census Abstract (PCA) 2011 | Total Population, Elderly ($60+$), Main/Marginal Agricultural & Construction Workers, Area/Density | Municipal Ward / Sub-district | Decennial (2011 baseline) | Open Government Data (OGD) India | Ward-level demographic vulnerability baseline weights | Decennial baseline (2011); does not reflect post-2011 urban sprawl or real-time migrant worker dynamics |
| **SRC-04** | National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC), MoHFW | [NCDC Climate Change & Health](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/includes/About/CentresAndDivision/CEOH.php) | National Action Plan for Heat-Related Illnesses (NAP-HRI) | Heat illness definitions, symptom progression, vulnerable groups | National / State guidelines | Annual guidance updates | Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Govt. of India | Grounding public health advisories for citizens, outdoor workers, and municipal health workers | Qualitative clinical & preventive guidance; not automated telemetry |
| **SRC-05** | National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC) | [NCDC Heat-Related Activities Report 2024](https://www.ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Report-of-Heat-Related-Activities-2024_NPCCHH.pdf) | NPCCHH Annual Heat Surveillance Report 2024 | Heat stroke cases, suspected heat mortalities (IHIP portal aggregates) | State / Sentinel Hospital aggregates | Annual Report (2024) | MoHFW / Public Domain | Justifying human health burden, epidemic surveillance validation, and SIH problem justification | Aggregate sentinel surveillance only; individual patient records restricted (Tier-3) |
| **SRC-06** | UTCI Management Committee / COST Action 730 | [UTCI Official Portal](https://www.utci.org/) | Universal Thermal Climate Index (Bröde et al., 2012) | UTCI equivalent temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$), thermal stress category | Point / Grid analytical formula | Peer-reviewed standard | Open Scientific Standard | Primary outdoor human physiological thermal stress calculation | 6th-order polynomial approximation valid within $-50^\circ\text{C} \le T_a \le +50^\circ\text{C}$ and $v_{10} \le 17\text{ m/s}$ |
| **SRC-07** | National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH / CDC) | [NIOSH Heat Stress Criteria](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/recommendations/) | NIOSH Criteria for a Recommended Standard (Pub No. 2016-106) | Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT), Work/Rest regimens, hydration benchmarks | Standard formulas | Peer-reviewed standard | US Public Domain | Occupational heat stress guidelines and mandatory rest schedules for outdoor laborers | Requires solar radiation / black globe temperature estimation when direct physical sensors are absent |
| **SRC-08** | World Health Organization (WHO) | [WHO Heat and Health Factsheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health) | WHO Heat-Health Guidance | Vulnerability determinants (elderly, chronic illness, outdoor work), physiological strain mechanisms | Global public health guidance | Periodic updates | WHO Open Access | Scientific justification of multi-criteria vulnerability weights and risk communication principles | High-level global policy guidance; adapted to Indian socio-economic realities |
| **SRC-09** | NCMRWF / MoES | [NCMRWF NWP Dashboard](https://nwp.ncmrwf.gov.in/forecast-dashboard/) | NCUM Unified Model Global & Regional Forecasts | 10-day numerical weather prediction fields | $12\text{ km}$ (Global NCUM), $4\text{ km}$ (Regional) | 4 cycles daily (00, 06, 12, 18 UTC) | Restricted MoES Data Policy (Tier-2) | Architecture specification for future operational forecast integration connector | Requires institutional credentials for programmatic binary GRIB/NetCDF streams; mapped via adapter stub |
| **SRC-10** | ISRO MOSDAC | [MOSDAC INSAT-3D/3DR Products](https://www.mosdac.gov.in/insat-3d-data-products) | INSAT-3D/3DR Meteorological Satellite Products | Land Surface Temperature (LST), Insolation, Total Precipitable Water | $1\text{ km} - 4\text{ km}$ spatial, 15-30 min temporal | Near-real-time satellite downlink | ISRO MOSDAC Open Registration (Tier-2) | Documented satellite high-resolution thermal refinement layer | Requires HMAC API signature and user account; represented as future Tier-2 connector |
| **SRC-11** | OpenStreetMap / Municipal GIS Repositories | [Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation / Open Data](https://github.com/datameet/Municipal_Spatial_Data) | Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) Ward Boundaries | Ward names, ward codes, polygon geometries | Ward / Zone level polygons | 2017/2020 administrative delineation | Open Database License (ODbL) | Ward boundary spatial visualization and demographic risk attribution | Administrative boundaries subject to periodic delimitation |
