# SIH26083 Requirement Traceability Matrix

This matrix maps every functional requirement and scientific mandate of Problem Statement SIH26083 to its concrete codebase implementation, verification test, and honest limitations.

| SIH Requirement | Codebase Module | Implementation Status | Direct Code / Test Evidence | Scientific / Technical Limitation |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Human Thermal Stress Index** | `backend/app/thermal/hazard.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Multi-index composite hazard calculation combining UTCI ($60\%$), WBGT ($25\%$), and Heat Index ($15\%$). Tested in `tests/test_hazard.py`. | Index models representative adult walking activity; individual metabolic disease/medication not modeled. |
| **2. Combined Temp, Humidity, Wind & Radiation** | `backend/app/thermal/utci.py`, `wbgt.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Mathematical formulation accepting $T_a$, $\text{RH}$, $v_{10}$, and all-sky shortwave solar flux $S$ ($\text{W/m}^2$). Validated in `tests/test_utci.py`. | Clamped to operational valid ranges ($v_{10} \ge 0.5\text{ m/s}$, $e \le 50\text{ hPa}$) per Bröde et al. (2012). |
| **3. Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI)** | `backend/app/thermal/utci.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Full 6th-order polynomial approximation (240+ terms) with Magnus-Tetens vapor pressure and Stefan-Boltzmann $T_{mrt}$. Validated against reference values in `tests/test_utci.py`. | Polynomial approximation error is $< 1.1^\circ\text{C}$ compared to 187-node Fiala differential system. |
| **4. Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT)** | `backend/app/thermal/wbgt.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Outdoor WBGT with Stull psychrometric natural wet-bulb and Liljegren radiation-adjusted black globe temperature. Tested in `tests/test_wbgt.py`. | Assumes standard cotton work clothing (1.0 clo); impermeable chemical suits elevate strain further. |
| **5. 3–5 Day Anticipation / Forecast Horizon** | `backend/app/data_sources/nasa_power.py`, `backend/app/api/endpoints.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Generates Day 1 through Day 5 daily forecast horizons with cumulative duration heatwave tracking. Verified in `tests/test_api.py`. | Uses Tier-1 automated meteorological trend ingestion; NCMRWF operational GRIB2 stream architected via `ncmrwf_stub.py`. |
| **6. Demographic Vulnerability (Elderly & Workers)** | `backend/app/vulnerability/demographic.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Ingests Census 2011 PCA ward statistics: elderly ($60+$) share, outdoor worker (agricultural/construction) share, and population density. Tested in `tests/test_vulnerability.py`. | Based on Census 2011 baseline data (decennial census limitation). |
| **7. GIS Mapping & Ward/Zone Level Risk** | `backend/app/gis/engine.py`, `frontend/js/map.js` | **Fully Implemented** | Generates Leaflet-compatible GeoJSON choropleth joining ward geometries with demographic risk attribution. Tested in `tests/test_gis.py`. | Represents ward-level risk attribution, NOT micro-scale ward-resolution meteorology. |
| **8. Actionable Public Health Advisories** | `backend/app/advisory/engine.py` | **Fully Implemented** | Persona-based rule engine generating specific, actionable guidance for (1) Citizens, (2) Outdoor Workers (NIOSH rest regimens), and (3) Municipal Authorities, grounded in NCDC 2024 & WHO standards. Tested in `tests/test_advisory.py`. | Advisory is decision-support advice; not personalized medical prescription. |
| **9. Mortality / Health Risk Concept** | `backend/app/risk/engine.py` | **Fully Implemented (as Relative Risk Score)** | Transparent, monotonic **Relative Heat-Health Risk Score (0–100)** classified into IMD 4-tier alert levels (Green, Yellow, Orange, Red). Tested in `tests/test_risk_engine.py`. | Does NOT claim ungrounded absolute death counts without protected hospital telemetry (Tier-3). |
| **10. REST API & Dashboard** | `backend/app/main.py`, `frontend/index.html` | **Fully Implemented** | 14 OpenAPI-documented endpoints, interactive Leaflet choropleth, Chart.js 5-day horizon charts, scenario demo runner. Tested in `tests/test_api.py`. | Web dashboard optimized for modern evergreen browsers. |
| **11. Future NCMRWF / MOSDAC Integration** | `backend/app/data_sources/ncmrwf_stub.py` | **Interface Defined (Tier-2)** | Documented Python abstract connector with schema contracts for operational GRIB2/LST binary streams. | Requires institutional MoES/ISRO credentials for live binary feeds. |
