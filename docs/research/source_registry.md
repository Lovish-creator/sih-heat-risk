# Research Database: Authoritative Source Registry

This document maintains the research bibliography supporting the scientific, epidemiological, meteorological, and technological choices in the SIH26083 implementation.

## 1. Meteorological & Forecast Agencies
- **India Meteorological Department (IMD)**: Heat Wave Guidance, Climatological Norms, Impact-Based Forecasting Guidelines. [https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/heatwave_guidance.php](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/heatwave_guidance.php)
- **National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting (NCMRWF)**: Unified Model (NCUM) Global and Regional forecast systems, MoES Government of India. [https://nwp.ncmrwf.gov.in/](https://nwp.ncmrwf.gov.in/)
- **NASA Langley Research Center**: POWER Project (Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resources) Hourly & Daily Meteorological and Solar APIs. [https://power.larc.nasa.gov/](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/)

## 2. Public Health & Occupational Agencies
- **National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC)**: National Action Plan for Heat-Related Illnesses (NAP-HRI), National Programme on Climate Change and Human Health (NPCCHH), MoHFW. [https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/)
- **National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH / CDC)**: Criteria for a Recommended Standard: Occupational Exposure to Heat and Hot Environments (DHHS/NIOSH Publication No. 2016-106). [https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/)
- **World Health Organization (WHO)**: Heat and Health Factsheet & Guidelines for Heat-Health Action Plans. [https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health)

## 4. Key Biometeorological & Epidemiological Peer-Reviewed Literature
1. **Bröde, P., et al. (2012)**: *Deriving the operational procedure for the Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI)*. International Journal of Biometeorology, 56(3), 481-494. DOI: 10.1007/s00484-011-0454-1.
2. **Stull, R. (2011)**: *Wet-Bulb Temperature from Relative Humidity and Air Temperature*. Journal of Applied Meteorology and Climatology, 50(11), 2267-2269. DOI: 10.1175/JAMC-D-11-0143.1.
3. **Rothfusz, L. P. (1990)**: *The Heat Index "Equation" (or, More Than You Ever Wanted to Know About Heat Index)*. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, Technical Attachment SR/SSD 90-23.
4. **Liljegren, J. C., et al. (2008)**: *Modeling the Wet Bulb Globe Temperature Using Standard Meteorological Measurements*. Journal of Occupational and Environmental Hygiene, 5(10), 645-655. DOI: 10.1080/15459620802310770.
5. **Azhar, G. S., et al. (2014)**: *Heat-Related Mortality in India: Excess All-Cause Mortality Associated with the 2010 Ahmedabad Heat Wave*. PLOS ONE, 9(3), e91831. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0091831.
6. **Gasparrini, A., et al. (2015)**: *Mortality risk attributable to high and low ambient temperature: a multicountry observational study*. The Lancet, 386(9991), 369-375. DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(14)62114-0.
7. **Kotharkar, R., & Ghosh, A. (2022)**: *Progress in extreme heat and urban thermal stress research in Indian cities: A review*. Urban Climate, 44, 101204.
