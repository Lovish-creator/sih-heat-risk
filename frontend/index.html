📐 Scientific Calculation & Weighting Framework

Methodology

Relative Heat-Health Risk ($R$) is synthesized using a calibrated multi-tier framework combining thermal hazard, socio-demographic vulnerability, and population exposure:

Relative Risk = (Hazard Score × 0.55) + (Demographic Vulnerability × 0.30) + (Heatwave Duration × 0.15)

Where Hazard combines 6th-order polynomial UTCI (Universal Thermal Climate Index COST 730), Liljegren/Stull WBGT, and NOAA Heat Index with microclimate Local Climate Zone (LCZ) adjustments, and Demographic Vulnerability = (Elderly 60+ × 0.40) + (Outdoor Laborers × 0.35) + (Population Density × 0.25).

⚠️ Prototype Decision-Support Limitations & Boundaries

Honest Boundaries
  • Relative Ranking, Not Mortality Forecast: Scores reflect comparative thermal stress and vulnerable population exposure, not clinical diagnoses or epidemiological mortality predictions.
  • Demographic Baseline: Demographic vulnerability utilizes Census of India 2011 Primary Census Abstract (PCA) tables as official public baseline data.
  • Weather Ingestion: Meteorological data is retrieved in real-time from Open-Meteo and NASA POWER open endpoints. Full production deployment envisions direct coupling with IMD / NCMRWF High-Resolution Regional Models.
